# Archive Review Queues

Adapt queue names to the collection. Keep each unresolved decision narrow enough that an archive
owner can act on it.

## `needs-placement`

The item belongs to this archive, but subject, entity, project, or lifecycle placement is unclear.
Retain original path, candidate destinations, and the signals supporting each candidate.

## `needs-date-or-subject`

Placement is broadly known, but a trustworthy discriminating date or subject is missing. Preserve
the original name until the missing fact is established.

## `same-subject-different-bytes`

Names or visible content suggest a relationship, but hashes differ. Classify as version, derivative,
variant, corruption, or unrelated material before naming or disposition.

## `bundle-or-link-review`

The item may be owned by an application bundle, sidecar relationship, relative link, sync service,
or generated library. Determine movement semantics before separating it.
