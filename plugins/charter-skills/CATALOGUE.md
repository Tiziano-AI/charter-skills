# Charter Skills catalogue

Choose the specialist that fits the work. If the need is hard to express, start with [Work Discovery](skills/charter-work-discovery/SKILL.md) to turn a concrete example into a brief.

## Use-case guides

Work Discovery's [seven guides](skills/charter-work-discovery/references/catalogue-routes.md) show how specialists can work together. Use them as starting points, not a fixed sequence.

## Specialists

### [3D Art & Visual Production](skills/charter-3d-and-visual-production/SKILL.md)

`charter-3d-and-visual-production` — Build or repair a 3D model or scene, produce an image or texture, or finish a still or film for its placement. Includes art direction, animation, render cost, crops, transparent assets, and playback. Not engine setup, gameplay rules, or designing the interface around the art.

### [Adaptive Communication](skills/charter-adaptive-communication/SKILL.md)

`charter-adaptive-communication` — Compose established meaning for its receiver, purpose and medium, reducing avoidable interpretation effort while preserving precision, relationships and expressive qualities.

### [Agent Tool Design](skills/charter-agent-tool-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-agent-tool-design` — Design CLIs, MCP servers and plugins or extensions that agents call: operations, identities, results, errors, host discovery, versions and what survives disable or uninstall, so callers act without reading source. Operating an existing tool follows its guide.

### [AI Evaluation & Agent Session Learning](skills/charter-ai-evaluation-and-session-learning/SKILL.md)

`charter-ai-evaluation-and-session-learning` — Learn from agent sessions: why a correction kept recurring or a result worked. Evaluate models, prompts and agents from actual outputs to decide what to adopt or repair. Not a transcript recap, live status check or prompt rewrite.

### [Architecture & Provider Design](skills/charter-architecture-and-provider-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-architecture-and-provider-design` — Simplify an architecture with native or upstream primitives, or design support and fallback across providers, models and backends. Decide which local responsibilities can go without losing capability. Not a local bug fix, account setup or service restart.

### [Archive Reorganization](skills/charter-archive-reorganization/SKILL.md)

`charter-archive-reorganization` — Reorganize file collections so people can find and interpret what they keep: choose names and structure, preserve bundles, versions and provenance, and plan recoverable moves. Not duplicate removal or disk reclamation.

### [Codebase Analysis & Change](skills/charter-codebase-analysis-and-change/SKILL.md)

`charter-codebase-analysis-and-change` — Assess an unfamiliar or changed repository, or carry a contract change across schemas, writers, readers and stored data. Trace what the product can do and where its behavior is decided. A known one-module fix needs no system assessment.

### [LLM Context Engineering](skills/charter-context-engineering/SKILL.md)

`charter-context-engineering` — Design or repair retrieval and context assembly for LLM answers: trace missing evidence, exceptions and conversation history through parsing, lookup, ranking and the actual request. Not a prompt rewrite or recovery of a running session.

### [Document Fidelity](skills/charter-document-fidelity/SKILL.md)

`charter-document-fidelity` — Make document properties survive export, import and editing in the recipient's application: Word pagination, slide masters, spreadsheet formulas, PDF reading order or extraction. Routine single-format authoring uses its format tool.

### [Game Design](skills/charter-game-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-game-design` — Design or improve how a game plays: rules, player choices, information, controls, pacing and progression. Use it for a paper game or an interactive scene, not for engine setup, visual polish or technical QA alone.

### [macOS Cleanup](skills/charter-macos-cleanup/SKILL.md)

`charter-macos-cleanup` — Mac disk cleanup: remove rebuildable caches, dependencies and build outputs without breaking active or source-linked consumers; reclaim already-free container storage. Not duplicates, models, histories, installed tools or data retirement.

### [Product Strategy & Design](skills/charter-product-strategy-and-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-product-strategy-and-design` — Choose whom a product should serve, design how its inputs become usable findings or actions, or improve onboarding, trust, recovery and return. Use when the market bet, substantive work or user journey is open, not for routine implementation of a settled design.

### [Prompt, Instruction & Skill Design](skills/charter-prompt-instruction-and-skill-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-prompt-instruction-and-skill-design` — Write, adapt or compare prompts for a model call or agent role; create a SKILL.md or fix a skill agents overlook; repair conflicting instructions or review guidance after a model or harness upgrade. Missing source facts need retrieval, not stronger wording.

### [Release Engineering & Configuration Diagnosis](skills/charter-release-and-configuration-diagnosis/SKILL.md)

`charter-release-and-configuration-diagnosis` — Ship a release to the consumer that should run it, or find why a configuration change has no effect. Trace the selected build or effective setting in the actual app, service, command or request. Diagnosing selection does not require a deploy.

### [Research & Decisions](skills/charter-research-and-decisions/SKILL.md)

`charter-research-and-decisions` — Verify a current or disputed fact, review the evidence in a field, or recommend a choice when uncertainty, competing preferences or shared commitments matter. A factual lookup ends with the answer; a routine implementation choice needs no decision study.

### [Agent Session Status, Coordination & Wind-Down](skills/charter-session-status-coordination-and-wind-down/SKILL.md)

`charter-session-status-coordination-and-wind-down` — Report status or readiness across agent sessions or tasks this runtime can list, coordinate a real dependency or collision, or wind work down before a pause, handoff or restart. A status question does not authorize messaging sessions; preparing to stop does not restart, resume or move them.

### [Stream Protocol Repair](skills/charter-stream-protocol-repair/SKILL.md)

`charter-stream-protocol-repair` — Repair streaming protocols when event semantics corrupt outcomes; fix framing, ordering, partial output, callbacks, cancellation, reconnect, replay or competing terminal signals at the responsible seam. Ordinary command waiting and recovery of one durable job are separate work.

### [System Failures & Mac Resource Diagnosis](skills/charter-system-and-mac-diagnosis/SKILL.md)

`charter-system-and-mac-diagnosis` — Diagnose an unexplained slowdown, recurring system failure, or hot, swapping or power-hungry Mac, even on the first occurrence. Trace the failing request, queue, process or loaded model before a restart erases the evidence. Not disk cleanup or profiling an already localized code path.

### [Software Test Design](skills/charter-test-design/SKILL.md)

`charter-test-design` — Software tests: adding tests, fixing a flaky one, or finding why a test passes or fails; choose expected values, boundaries and mocks that catch the real bug, and drop tests that protect nothing.

### [Web, Apple & Status Interfaces](skills/charter-web-apple-and-status-interfaces/SKILL.md)

`charter-web-apple-and-status-interfaces` — Design a website, web app or native Apple interface, or fix misleading progress, outcomes and controls in a GUI or CLI. Covers editorial pages, working screens, accessible input, responsive layout, Apple windows and commands, and terminal-only status. Reporting a stuck operation is not recovering it.

### [Work Discovery](skills/charter-work-discovery/SKILL.md)

`charter-work-discovery` — Clarify a need the person cannot yet put into a request: use a concrete episode, evidence or contrasting examples to discover the intended result and carry it through. A clear request needs no interview.

### [Interrupted Workflow Recovery](skills/charter-workflow-recovery/SKILL.md)

`charter-workflow-recovery` — Recover one interrupted job, batch or external operation without duplicating a send, charge or other uncertain effect. Reconcile by operation identity, not a lost connection; a healthy running job needs no recovery.

Adaptive Communication is selectable, on-demand guidance. This library does not install Charter's standing instructions, runtime adapters or custom roles.
